The cast. Arjun — a software engineer in Chennai who wants a car loan. Priya — the company's Super Admin, the only person who logs into Platform Admin. Ramesh — a Direct Selling Executive (DSE) at the Anna Nagar branch, who uses the downstream Kyber AI LOS app to process Arjun's application. The systems: UMS (authoritative for people), the Authentication Service, Kyber AI LOS (the downstream lending app), BRE (the Business Rules Engine) and CarTrade (the vehicle data provider).

Phase 0 — Priya keeps the reference data ready §11–§16

Months before Arjun applies, Priya maintains the data the loan engine will need:

  • MMV (§12): the car Arjun will buy — Make · Model · Variant — e.g. Maruti · Grand Vitara · Zeta 1.5L — uploaded via Excel with its CarTrade Variant Mapping.
  • Location (§13): Chennai's geography — Zone (South) → Region (Tamil Nadu) → Area (Chennai Metro) → Branch (Anna Nagar); State (Tamil Nadu) → City (Chennai) → Pincode 600040; plus the mapping from Internal City “Chennai” to a stable CarTrade City ID.
  • Price (§15): the exact price for (Variant + CarTrade City “Chennai” + Charge “processing fee”). One exact number — no fallback.
  • Negative Area (§14): rules marking risky localities — e.g. one nearby street is WATCHLIST → EXCEPTION_REVIEW.
  • Early Delinquency (§16): last month's default data for the Anna Nagar branch, uploaded and published as a Ready Readiness Version for the BRE.
  • Common Master Data (§11): the Approval Workflow that routes the loan, the Charge catalogue, Schemes (a festive offer), PDC Bank, and more.

Phase 1 — Ramesh gets access to the LOS §10

Ramesh can't process anything until he can log into Kyber AI LOS. Priya opens User Management (§10), searches Ramesh — UMS says he's an active, eligible DSE at the Anna Nagar branch. She sees his 11 independent status dimensions all green, selects Initial Activation, picks the reason “Initial Access Grant”, writes remarks, and confirms. A Pending Audit Intent is recorded first; UMS confirms; the change is enforced downstream within 60 seconds; Ramesh gets an email within 5 minutes. Every bit of this lands in the Audit Log (§17).

Phase 2 — Arjun applies §8 §14 §13 §15 §12

Arjun walks into the branch. Ramesh logs into the LOS (his is the downstream app — Priya's own portal login is §8) and enters Arjun's details. The engine now consults the data Priya prepared:

  • Negative Area (§14): Arjun's locality is normalized and matched — it's a clean area → NO_MATCH, so the application proceeds. (If it were WATCHLIST, it would route to Credit Risk Exception Review instead.)
  • MMV (§12): the exact Variant is resolved.
  • Location → Price (§13.5, §15): Pincode 600040 → City Chennai → Internal City Chennai → CarTrade City ID → one exact Price.
  • Approval Workflow (§11): the configured workflow routes the application by amount / LTV / tenure conditions → APPROVE or REFER.

Phase 3 — The engine decides §16

To set the risk terms, the BRE reads the Anna Nagar branch's ED Ready Readiness Version (§16) — never a partial month, never the previous month as a fallback. Combined with the exact Price, the Negative Area result and the Approval Workflow, the loan is approved.

Phase 4 — Priya keeps watch §9 §17 §19

Priya's Dashboard (§9) shows the status of her ED / Price / MMV uploads, alerts, and recent activity. If anything looks off — a source-disabled user still enabled, an upload Partially Completed — it raises an Action Required or an alert. She can search the Audit Log (§17) for any action, hers or the system's, and export reports (§19) — each requiring an Export Reason and leaving its own audit trail.

Phase 5 — A late payment, corrected §16

A month later the Anna Nagar branch reports a missed payment, so that branch's ED number for the Applicable Month is wrong. Priya uploads a CORRECT row (after cut-off, with Credit Risk approval). The month goes Ready → Correction Pending → a new Ready Readiness Version; the old version stays serving the BRE until the new one is ready atomically. The correction is immutable, history is preserved, and the BRE consumes the new version.

Why this story sticks: Arjun never touches Platform Admin. The portal exists to keep the data and the access correct so the downstream Kyber AI LOS can make a safe, exact decision — and to prove, in an immutable audit trail, exactly how that decision was made.

§1

Executive Summary

The Platform Admin portal is the secure, controlled interface for administering the reference and operational data consumed by the Kyber AI LOS Platform.

It administers LOS User Access, business masters, vehicle data, organizational and geographical mappings, Negative Area rules, Price data and monthly Early Delinquency data — plus an operational Dashboard, immutable audit evidence, upload reconciliation, version history and controlled exports.

Current-phase control model

  • One interactive portal role — Super Admin — provisioned outside the portal.
  • No maker-checker workflow anywhere. Control comes from live validation, mandatory change reasons, external references for governed actions, effective dating, immutable versioning, durable Pending Audit Intent before every change, dependency validation and complete upload reconciliation.
  • UMS (User Management Service) stays authoritative for identity, employment, account, role, Business Function, organization and eligibility. Platform Admin controls LOS User Access only — never a user repository, never grants access to itself.
  • Published or consumed data cannot be overwritten or deleted; corrections create new versions and downstream systems retain the exact Record ID + Version used.
  • MMV and Price are Excel-only. Price resolves on one exact combination of stable Variant Record ID + stable CarTrade City ID + stable Charge Record ID — no fallback of any kind.
  • ED is maintained by Branch and Applicable Month and reaches the Business Rules Engine (BRE) only through a complete, immutable Ready Readiness Version.

Success measures

  1. Every business-changing action is validated, reasoned, versioned and auditable.
  2. An ineligible UMS user cannot be granted LOS User Access.
  3. A consumed record cannot be overwritten or permanently deleted.
  4. Price resolution returns one exact valid record or raises Price Required.
  5. BRE receives ED data only from a complete Ready Readiness Version.
  6. Upload totals reconcile exactly, with downloadable evidence for every rejected or unprocessed row.
  7. No timeout, unknown outcome or dependency failure ever produces a fabricated success.

§6

Cross-Module Business Rules

These rules apply to every module unless a module defines a stricter rule. Stated once, not repeated per module.

6.1 Record lifecycle

StatusMeaningPermitted next states
DraftUnconsumed editable versionScheduled, Active or Discarded
ScheduledPublished future-effective version or actionActive, Superseded, Inactive or Cancelled
ActiveCurrently applicable versionSuperseded (replacement), or Inactive (controlled deactivation)
InactiveUnavailable for new use but retained historicallyNo direct reactivation — a new version is required
SupersededImmutable historical version replaced by a later versionNone
DiscardedWithdrawn unconsumed DraftNone

Published is a milestone, not a status. A scheduled action is a controlled record with its own immutable identity and version.

6.2 Effective dating

  • Effective From is inclusive; Effective To is inclusive through 11:59:59 PM IST; future activation at 12:00:00 AM IST on Effective From.
  • Blank Effective To is open-ended. Future dating permitted up to 365 days unless a module rule differs.
  • Normal backdating prohibited; retrospective correction needs owner authorization, external reference and impact analysis, and never recalculates completed downstream records.
  • Same-day activation takes effect at confirmed processing time. Overlapping applicability for the same business key and context is prohibited.

6.3 Versioning and immutability

  • New logical record → new Record ID + Business Code. Versions start at 1, increase sequentially.
  • MODIFY applies only to an unconsumed Draft. CORRECT creates the next version with complete before/after evidence and revalidated dependencies.
  • Published, Active, Scheduled, Superseded and consumed versions are immutable.
  • Reactivation of an Inactive record creates a new version; the historical Inactive version is never switched back.
  • Downstream consumers retain the exact Record ID + Version Identifier used. Permanent deletion of published/consumed/historical records is prohibited platform-wide.

6.4 Discarded Draft retention

Full payload of a Discarded Draft remains available for 90 days, then is securely removed. Minimal audit evidence remains for seven years: Record ID, module/master, Business Code, Draft version, actor, created/discarded timestamps, reason, external reference where present, and a cryptographic payload hash.

6.5 Dependency-controlled deactivation

Before deactivation the portal identifies Active/Scheduled children, mappings, Price records and mandatory process dependencies. A blocking dependency prevents submission; a warning requires acknowledgement + Change Reason. No automatic cascade. Scheduled deactivation revalidates dependencies immediately before execution.

6.6 Search, filter, history and export

  • Text search is case-insensitive, trims whitespace, prioritizes exact code/identifier matches. Filters combine AND across categories, OR within a category; search + filters combine with AND. Default pagination 25 (options 25/50/100). Current = latest state; History = every authorized version.
  • Every export: mandatory Export Reason, authorized fields only, masking/exclusion of restricted & unnecessary personal fields, retained filter metadata, stamped classification, audited on generation and download. Authenticated download link single-user, expires in 24 hours. Over 10,000 rows → asynchronous. Max 100,000 records/file unless a module limit is lower.
  • Credentials, secrets, tokens and unrestricted before/after security payloads are never exportable.

6.7 Screen states

Every screen and Dashboard card defines Loading, No Data, Partially Available, Stale, Service Unavailable and Action Required behaviour. Failure of one dependency never prevents unrelated navigation. Service failures show a safe message + correlation reference — never technical detail or stack traces.

6.8 Concurrency

The platform protects against duplicate submissions, concurrent updates to the same record, stale record versions, concurrent upload conflicts, stale schedule execution and duplicate source events. Optimistic version checking (or equivalent) preserves the latest valid business state.

6.9 Date, time and timezone

  • UI displays IST: DD/MM/YYYY and DD/MM/YYYY hh:mm:ss AM/PM IST. Interfaces exchange UTC ISO 8601.
  • Browser timezone never alters a business-effective date, schedule result or access calculation.
  • Monetary values use ₹ with Indian digit grouping and field-approved precision.

6.10 Acceptance criteria

  1. Attempting to modify a consumed version is rejected and does not alter history.
  2. A correction creates the next version and retains the earlier version unchanged.
  3. A conflicting effective period is rejected before publication.
  4. A blocking dependency prevents deactivation and identifies the affected records.
  5. A valid scheduled action revalidates status, version and dependencies before execution.
  6. A failed audit-intent write blocks the business change.
  7. Export includes only authorized fields and creates a durable audit event.

7.2 Template governance

Each template carries Template Name, Version, Published Date and Effective Date. Only the current template is downloadable. During a five-business-day transition the immediately previous template is accepted until its exact retirement timestamp; a batch accepted before retirement continues under its accepted version. After retirement, upload is rejected with UPLOAD-TEMPLATE-INVALID. Template structure is never silently converted.

7.3 File-level controls (reject the whole file before row processing)

Unsupported format; encrypted or password-protected workbook; macros; external links; executable or embedded objects; formulas in business-data cells; hidden business-data rows/columns; merged business-data cells; unreadable workbook; altered worksheet structure/names/order; missing required worksheet; altered mandatory column structure. Only literal business values are accepted.

7.4 Approved actions

ADD, CORRECT, ACTIVATE, DEACTIVATE. MODIFY applies only to an unconsumed Draft where the module supports Draft maintenance. Status is derived by the system, never entered in the workbook. Business codes are immutable and cannot be reused.

  • MMV & Price: ADD publishes an immediate Active or future Scheduled version directly — no editable screen Draft.
  • ED: ADD creates Version 1 in monthly staging; it becomes Active only through readiness processing.
  • Masters with manual maintenance: ADD creates a Draft; ACTIVATE publishes it.

7.5 Validation sequence

  1. File format and structure controls
  2. Template and worksheet contract
  3. Column contract
  4. Row-level field validation
  5. Business-key and duplicate validation
  6. Reference and dependency validation, in dependency order
  7. Effective-period and overlap validation
  8. Commit under Pending Audit Intent

Failure at stages 1–3 rejects the complete workbook; failures at stages 4–7 are row-level.

7.6 Row outcomes

Total Records = Successful + Failed + Duplicate + Unprocessed — mutually exclusive categories.

  • Successful — committed.
  • Failed — processed but rejected by a validation or business rule.
  • Duplicate — exact duplicate input per module rules; never also counted as Failed.
  • Unprocessed — not reached because of timeout, stop or a failed prerequisite.

Multiple actions against the same logical key or version within one batch are Failed as conflicting.

7.7 Batch status model

StatusMeaning
AcceptedFile received and Batch ID issued.
RejectedFile-level control failure; no rows processed.
In ProgressRow processing under way.
CompletedAll rows processed; every row Successful.
Partially CompletedAll rows processed; at least one row not Successful.
Timed OutProcessing exceeded module timeout; committed rows retained.
Reconciliation PendingOutcome or audit finalization requires controlled reconciliation.
StoppedProcessing halted on request; remaining rows Unprocessed.

Partially Completed is the approved term. Reconciliation is a separate status dimension, never merged into processing status.

7.8 Partial success, retry, resume, reprocess, stop

  • Where row-level processing is permitted, successful independent rows remain committed; one row's failure never rolls back unrelated successful rows; dependent rows remain Unprocessed where their prerequisite failed.
  • Retry / Resume reuse the original Batch ID, checksum and idempotency context; committed rows are never committed again.
  • Reprocess is a new business action — new Batch ID, captured reason, retained source Batch reference.
  • Stop leaves committed rows committed, marks remaining rows Unprocessed, audits the stop reason.

7.9 Validation report

Identifies Batch ID, worksheet, row number, action, business key, field, submitted value, result category, validation code and description, dependency failure, target Record ID and Version, and processing outcome. Lets the Super Admin correct failed rows and submit a controlled new attempt.

7.10 Acceptance criteria

  1. An accepted submission receives a Batch ID and acknowledgement within five seconds under normal service health.
  2. A file-level control failure rejects the complete file before row processing.
  3. Batch totals always satisfy the reconciliation equation.
  4. Partial-success modules preserve committed rows and report every non-successful row independently.
  5. A timed-out batch retains committed rows, classifies the remainder Unprocessed and enters reconciliation.
  6. Retry or Resume never duplicates a committed row; Reprocess creates a new Batch ID.
  7. Failure to create Pending Audit Intent prevents processing.
  8. Final audit failure after commit retains the business result and sets Reconciliation Pending.
Module §8

Login, Authentication, Profile & Session

Authenticate the Super Admin through the Authentication Service; the portal itself never touches the authentication database.

📖 In the story: Priya's own portal login runs on exactly these rules — one session, 20-minute idle, 8-hour cap. Ramesh's login is the downstream Kyber AI LOS app, not this portal.

8.1 Login

Login page contains mandatory Login ID, Password, a Login action and Forgot Password.

FieldRule
Login IDRequired; 3–50 chars; letters, numbers, period, hyphen, underscore; case-insensitive; leading/trailing spaces removed; embedded spaces prohibited. @ is not permitted, so email is not a valid Login ID.
PasswordRequired; masked during entry; submitted only to the Authentication Service.

The portal never accesses the authentication database and never stores, displays or logs passwords.

8.2 Authentication Service handling

Portal waits max 10 seconds, never retries automatically. A valid success response + token is required before a session is created. Unavailable service, timeout, malformed or incomplete response is a service failure and does not increment the failed-login counter. Technical detail is logged with a correlation ID, never displayed.

8.3 Failed login & blocking

  • Account blocked after 3 consecutive invalid-password attempts. Counter resets only on successful auth or authorized manual unblock. Block has no automatic expiry.
  • Authorized Support Administrator verifies identity and unblocks; the Super Admin cannot unblock their own account. Existing password stays valid after unblock; a temporary password is not issued merely because the account was blocked.
  • Concurrent invalid attempts update one atomic counter. Service failures and timeouts never count as invalid-password attempts.

8.4 Multi-factor authentication

MFA is not applicable — no OTP or authenticator validation, enrolment or recovery. Successful Login ID + password is sufficient, subject to account status, authorization and session controls.

8.5 Forgot Password & temporary credentials

Forgot Password is informational only: displays approved Support instructions, creates no reset link/token/OTP/email/form. Reset occurs through the approved Support process. A temporary password opens a restricted password-change session (password change only, no Dashboard/module access), valid 24 hours or until changed. Reissue invalidates the prior temporary credential.

8.7 Change Password

Available from Profile. Auth Service verifies the current password and applies the approved enterprise password policy. On success, all existing sessions are revoked, tokens invalidated, reauthentication required. Password values never appear in the Audit Log.

8.8 Session controls

Tokens

Access token 10 minutes. Refresh token ≤8 hours from original login. Renewal rotates and invalidates the preceding refresh token. Background polling never renews a session. Refresh tokens in secure HTTP-only cookies (never local storage). Complete tokens never displayed, logged or exported.

Inactivity & absolute duration

Session expires after 20 minutes without qualifying server-side activity (warning at 18 min, 2-min countdown). Qualifying: opening a module/record, search, filter, sort, page change, save, submit, upload, authorized download, Continue Session. Non-qualifying: mouse movement, passive scrolling, unsaved typing, open page, background refresh, activity in another app. Absolute limit 8 hours, cannot be extended. First applicable limit wins; absolute expiry takes precedence. Continue Session not offered when it can't extend beyond the absolute limit.

Concurrency & browser closure

One active session; multiple tabs in one browser session are one session. Successful login elsewhere revokes the prior session (unsuccessful attempts do not). Closing the browser is not Logout and restores nothing on reopen; orphaned session expires at earlier of inactivity/absolute limit.

Immediate termination

All tokens/sessions revoked on: Change Password, account blocking/deactivation, access removal, inactive/suspended/terminated/revoked identity, disabled/replaced Login ID, backend password reset, temporary-password issuance/reissue, authorized manual revocation, qualifying security incident, login elsewhere, refresh-token reuse, inactivity/absolute expiry, or Logout.

Logout

Detects unsaved changes, shows confirmation, revokes tokens/session, clears browser auth + sensitive session data, terminates all tabs, redirects to Login, creates an audit event. Cancel preserves session/unsaved changes; continuing discards them without submitting. A submitted upload with a Batch ID continues processing; a committed action is never rolled back by session expiry.

16.3 Month & Branch controls

  1. Normal submission is for the immediately preceding month.
  2. Controlled corrections may cover the previous 12 Applicable Months; older corrections require Credit Risk Owner approval + external reference.
  3. A workbook may contain a partial set of Branches, but the assembled monthly dataset is a complete snapshot.
  4. Every Branch active for any instant during the Applicable Month requires one valid ED record. A Branch activated after month-end is excluded; one deactivated during/after the month remains required if active for any part of it.
  5. At month-end, Location Service creates an immutable Branch Eligibility Snapshot (each qualifying Branch version, effective interval, organizational path); Snapshot ID + version retained with the monthly control record.
  6. A later hierarchy correction does not silently change the snapshot; a snapshot correction creates a new version with Credit Risk approval.

16.4 Cut-off & receipt

Standard cut-off is 9:00 PM IST on the fifth working day (Corporate India Business Calendar, excluding configured weekends/holidays). At 12:00:00 AM IST on the first calendar day after the Applicable Month, the calendar version + calculated cut-off are frozen in the monthly control record. Exceptional recalculation creates a new control version with Operations Calendar Owner + Credit Risk Owner approval.

The upload gateway issues an immutable Submission Receipt on receipt of the complete file + checksum; its timestamp determines cut-off compliance.

  • Timely receipt preserves on-time treatment where the file is otherwise valid but a platform-controlled outage delays Batch creation/processing.
  • Timely receipt grants no after-cut-off resubmission for a corrupt/encrypted/obsolete-template/structurally-altered/otherwise user-invalid file.
  • Outage evidence, original receipt + checksum, and incident reference are mandatory; a protected resubmission uses the same checksum unless Support requires a regenerated file explicitly linked to the incident.
  • Where outage prevents a receipt, the Credit Risk Owner may approve an extension equal to the verified outage duration (audited with incident reference).
  • After cut-off: a missing first record follows Late Initial Addition (ADD — no logical record exists — with row reason, batch reason, Credit Risk approval); an existing record follows controlled CORRECT.

16.6.1 Correction transition (Closed → Correction Pending → Ready → Closed)
  1. Closed → Correction Pending. Open Controlled Correction requires Credit Risk Owner approval + reference. Prior Ready Readiness Version and Month Closure Version remain intact and available to BRE throughout.
  2. Correction Pending → Ready. On successful complete correction a new Readiness Version is created and becomes Ready atomically; only at that instant does BRE begin consuming it. Prior Ready version becomes Superseded and is retained.

16.7 Concurrency

Branch ID + Applicable Month locked by the first accepted processing row. Lock wait up to 5 minutes; unresolved conflict → Unprocessed / Key Locked. Valid rows may enter staging independently; failed/duplicate rows do not roll back unrelated valid rows, but the month stays Incomplete until all required Branches are valid. The completeness report identifies required, received, missing, failed, duplicate and pending Branches.

16.8 BRE consumption

  1. BRE uses the latest Active Ready Readiness Version at its evaluation timestamp.
  2. Previous-month fallback is prohibited.
  3. No eligible record for the required Branch + month → decision stops with workflow exception status ED Required, no derived value, High Action Required item.
  4. BRE and the application retain ED Record ID, Version Number, Branch ID, Applicable Month, ED Value, % ED, Readiness Version, Branch Snapshot version, evaluation timestamp.
  5. Later corrections never overwrite evidence retained by an existing evaluation.
  6. A Credit Risk Approver or authorized Operations user may request re-evaluation with application reference, reason and Credit Risk approval; both evaluations retained.

16.9 Search & reporting

Search: Branch ID, Branch Name, Applicable Month, Batch ID, Record ID. Filters: row status, month-readiness state, correction status, cut-off status, effective/processing period. Current, history and version-comparison views required. Monthly reconciliation export: Applicable Month, Snapshot ID + version, required/received/missing Branches, row versions, Batch IDs, status counts, readiness version, closure version, cut-off evidence, unresolved items.

16.10 Acceptance criteria

  1. A workbook with more than one Applicable Month is rejected before row processing.
  2. Only the exact ED_DATA worksheet and approved column structure are accepted, with action-specific mandatory-field validation.
  3. Excel percentage values and numeric fractions are interpreted per the approved rules; plain values outside 0 to 1 are rejected.
  4. Completeness uses the immutable Branch Eligibility Snapshot and includes every Branch active for any part of the month.
  5. A valid Submission Receipt issued by the cut-off preserves on-time treatment only under the approved platform-controlled delay rules.
  6. A late first record uses Late Initial Addition; an existing record uses controlled CORRECT, each with required reasons and approval evidence.
  7. A correction never removes the prior Ready version from BRE until the replacement Readiness Version becomes Ready atomically.
  8. A failed or abandoned correction creates no partial Readiness Version, leaves the prior Ready version + closure evidence unchanged and raises Action Required identifying the failed rows.
  9. Reclosure creates a new immutable Month Closure Version and retains the prior one.
  10. Deactivation is immediate, screen-controlled and limited to an unconsumed erroneous version; a consumed version cannot be deactivated.
  11. BRE never uses previous-month fallback and retains the exact evidence consumed.
  12. The Batch summary reconciles mutually exclusive counts to Total Records.
